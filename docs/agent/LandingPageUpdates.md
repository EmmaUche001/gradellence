# GRADELLENCE LANDING PAGE

## Early Adopter Conversion & UI/UX Design Specification. This is an update, I do not say start all over from what we have at the landingPage, but to update and extend. For example the Hero needs one change , the "See how it works" button beside "Get Early Access" should be "View Partnership Proposal" Use existing Gradellence visual identity and design system, UI components and animations.

**Product:** Gradellence
**Product Type:** Cloud-based School Result Management System
**Target Users:** Nigerian private school proprietors, directors, administrators and academic staff
**Campaign:** Pre-launch Early Adopter Programme
**Official Launch:** End of September 2026

---

# 1. PURPOSE OF THIS UPDATE

The Gradellence landing page should no longer function primarily as a conventional waitlist page.

The current pre-launch objective is to:

1. Generate awareness.
2. Explain what Gradellence does.
3. Demonstrate the product visually.
4. Build trust.
5. Present the Early Adopter opportunity.
6. Convert interested schools into committed Early Adopters.
7. Collect pre-launch revenue that funds the final launch.
8. Build a direct relationship with participating schools before launch.
9. Convert interested but undecided schools into demo conversations.

The landing page should therefore guide visitors through:

**Discover → Understand → Trust → Evaluate → Commit**

# 5. VIDEO / PRODUCT DEMONSTRATION

The hero video should be approximately:

**15–30 seconds**

It should show the product in action rather than explain everything verbally.

Suggested sequence:

```text
Dashboard
   ↓
Teacher enters scores
   ↓
Result computation
   ↓
Result review
   ↓
Report card generation
   ↓
Parent/student result access
```

The video should be muted by default with optional playback controls.

A subtle play button should sit over the video.

Avoid making the video feel like a YouTube embed.

It should feel like part of the SaaS product interface.

# 6. PARTNERSHIP PROPOSAL

The existing Partnership Proposal should become an important supporting sales asset.

The landing page should provide access to:

**View Partnership Proposal**

and, elsewhere on the page:

**Download the Early Adopter Proposal**

The PDF should open in a new browser tab.

Do not force visitors to download the document before they can view it.

Recommended behavior:

```text
Click "View Partnership Proposal"
              ↓
Open PDF in new tab
```

On mobile, the PDF should use the browser's native document viewer.

# 8. IMPORTANT PAYMENT LANGUAGE

This is extremely important.

The website must make it clear that the school is paying before launch, but its subscription period does not begin immediately.

Avoid ambiguous wording such as:

> Start your subscription today.

Instead use:

> **Secure your Early Adopter spot today. Your subscription starts when Gradellence officially launches.**

Another possible explanation:

> Your Early Adopter payment secures your school's place in the programme. Your subscription period begins when Gradellence officially launches at the end of September 2026.

This should appear directly beside the payment CTA.

# 19. WHEN TO USE WHATSAPP

WhatsApp should not be the primary infrastructure for the entire funnel.

It should be the **human layer**.

Use it for:

* Questions
* Demo coordination
* Payment assistance
* Plan discussions
* Onboarding
* Follow-up
* Schools that prefer bank transfer
* Schools that are interested but not ready to pay online

Recommended CTA:

**Chat With Gradellence**

rather than:

**Pay via WhatsApp**

The website should remain the primary conversion platform.

# 23. EARLY ADOPTER URGENCY

The scarcity should be based on available programme slots.

Do not use an artificial countdown timer unless there is a genuine deadline.

> **Limited Early Adopter slots available.**

> The Early Adopter Programme remains open only until the available slots are filled.

Once the slots are filled, the Early Adopter offer closes.

# 28. MOBILE UX

The landing page must be designed mobile-first.

Important considerations:

* Hero CTA buttons should stack vertically if necessary.
* Proposal button should remain easily accessible.
* Product video should maintain a proper aspect ratio.
* Pricing cards should stack vertically.
* Early Adopter benefits should use compact cards/list items.
* Forms should use large touch-friendly fields.
* Payment flow must work cleanly on mobile.
* WhatsApp CTA can remain visible as a secondary floating/contact action.

---

# 29. MICROCOPY

Avoid overly technical language.

Instead of:

> Enterprise-grade academic information management infrastructure.

Prefer:

> Manage your school's results faster, more accurately and securely.

Instead of:

> Automated academic computation engine.

Prefer:

> Let Gradellence handle the result computation.

Instead of:

> Multi-role access control.

Prefer:

> Give administrators, teachers, parents and students the right access.

# 32. MOST IMPORTANT BUSINESS PRINCIPLE

The landing page should not try to force every visitor into payment.

There are three levels of intent:

### LOW INTENT

Visitor is curious.

→ Watch demo
→ Read website

### MEDIUM INTENT

Visitor is seriously considering Gradellence.

→ View proposal
→ Book demo
→ WhatsApp

### HIGH INTENT

Visitor has decided.

→ Get Early Access
→ Select plan
→ Pay
→ Secure Early Adopter spot

The landing page should accommodate all three.
